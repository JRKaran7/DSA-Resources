Notes & Key Terms

Key Terms

  • Neuron: takes inputs, weights them, adds bias, applies activation.
  • Weight: strength of a connection; learned.
  • Bias: shifts the neuron's output; learned.
  • Activation: adds non-linearity (ReLU, Tanh, Sigmoid).
  • Forward pass: inputs → prediction.
  • Loss: how wrong the prediction is (MSE).
  • Backprop: sends error backward to find each weight's blame.
  • Gradient: direction that increases the loss.
  • Learning rate: step size of each update.
  • Epoch: one full pass over the dataset.
  • Overfitting: memorizes training data; L2 helps.

Remember While Explaining

  • Training loop: forward → loss → backward → update, repeat.
  • Without activations, the network is just a linear model.
  • Too high a learning rate makes the loss bounce or diverge; too low is slow.
  • XOR needs a hidden layer; a single neuron cannot solve it.
  • Sigmoid can make gradients vanish; ReLU usually trains faster.
  • Watch the loss curve falling and the decision boundary reshaping.

Key Learning Exercises

  • Forward Dimensions: Verify $Z_1 = W_1 \cdot X + b_1$ matrix math: with 2 inputs and 4 hidden neurons, $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{4 \times 2}$.
  • Non-Linear Challenge: Train on the XOR dataset. Notice how with 4 hidden neurons and ReLU, it solves non-linear XOR in under 200 epochs!
  • Gradient Vanishing Test: Switch activation from ReLU to Sigmoid and observe how convergence speed changes.
